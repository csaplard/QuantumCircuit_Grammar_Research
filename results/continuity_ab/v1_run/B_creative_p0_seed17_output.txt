As the sun began to set on the rocky coast, the lighthouse keeper, Mr. Jenkins, stood at the edge of the cliff, his eyes scanning the horizon for any signs of danger. The waves crashed against the shore, and the night air was filled with the salty tang of the sea. But as he turned to look at the tower in the distance, he noticed something strange. There was a small, unmarked envelope stuck to the windbreak on the wall. Curiosity piqued, he picked it up and unwrapped it.

Inside was a simple letter, its envelope barely big enough to hold any paper. The handwriting was neat and composed, and the sender's name was clearly visible: "To the Keeper of the Lighthouse." 

Mr. Jenkins felt a chill run down his spine as he read the words: "Please find me at the old church on the outskirts of town. I have news that will change your life."
