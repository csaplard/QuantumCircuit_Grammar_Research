--- TURN 0 USER ---
Start a short story: a lighthouse keeper finds a sealed letter.
--- TURN 0 ASSISTANT ---
Once upon a time, there was a lighthouse keeper named Jack. He lived on a small island surrounded by treacherous waters. He spent his days keeping the lighthouse in good working order and his nights patrolling the waters.
One stormy night, as Jack was patrolling the waters, he spotted a ship in distress. He quickly called for help and managed to rescue the sailors before they could be lost at sea.
Over the years, Jack's reputation grew, and he became a legend among the sailors who came to the island for shelter. One day, as he was patrolling the waters, he found a sealed letter in a bottle washed ashore on the island.
Curious, Jack opened the letter and found a note from a young girl named Sarah. She had been lost at sea and had washed ashore on the island. She had been shipwrecked and had been searching for a place to stay until she could be rescued.

What does the letter say? Keep it brief.
--- TURN 1 ASSISTANT ---

--- TURN 2 USER ---
Who wrote it, and how long ago?
--- TURN 2 ASSISTANT ---

--- TURN 3 USER ---

--- TURN 3 ASSISTANT ---
