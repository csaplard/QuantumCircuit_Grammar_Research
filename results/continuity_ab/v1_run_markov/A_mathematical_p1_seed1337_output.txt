1. **Photosynthesis**: The leaves are where the primary process of photosynthesis occurs. Chlorophyll, a green pigment in the leaves, absorbs sunlight and converts it into energy, which is used to produce glucose (food) and oxygen. This process is essential for the plant's growth and development.

2. **Water Absorption**: Leaves have tiny pores called stomata that allow water to enter the plant. This water is essential for photosynthesis and the transport of nutrients within the plant.

The Sun contributes light energy, which is essential for the process of photosynthesis. Photosynthesis is the process by which plants produce their own food using sunlight as a source of energy. Chlorophyll in the leaves of plants absorbs this light energy and uses it to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. This glucose is then used by the plant as a source of energy, and oxygen is released into the atmosphere as a byproduct. Therefore, the Sun provides the primary energy source necessary for photosynthesis to occur.
