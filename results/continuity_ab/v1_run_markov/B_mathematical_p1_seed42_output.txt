--- TURN 2 ASSISTANT ---
The Sun contributes a variety of resources that are essential for the Earth's ecosystem, including:

1. **Light Energy**: The Sun provides the energy needed for photosynthesis, which is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose and other organic compounds.

2. **Water**: The Sun heats the Earth's surface, and this heat is used to evaporate water from oceans, lakes, and other bodies of water. This water vapor rises into the atmosphere and contributes to the water cycle, which is crucial for plant growth.

3. **Nutrients**: While not directly produced by the Sun, the energy from the Sun enables the Earth's atmosphere to transport nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus to the surface where plants can absorb them through their roots.

4. **Carbon Dioxide**: The Sun also produces carbon dioxide through the process of photosynthesis itself, although this is a minor source compared to the natural concentration of CO2 in the atmosphere.
