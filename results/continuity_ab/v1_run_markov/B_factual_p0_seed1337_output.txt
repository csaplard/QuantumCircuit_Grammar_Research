Strong tides are a natural phenomenon that occurs when the gravitational pull of the Moon and Sun is aligned with the Earth, causing the ocean to bulge slightly on both sides of the Earth. This results in higher-than-normal sea levels, known as high tides, and lower-than-normal sea levels, known as low tides. The times when these tides occur are often referred to as "spring tides" because the gravitational forces of the Moon and Sun combine to create the strongest tidal effects.
