**1. The Ingredients:**

*   **Carbon Dioxide (CO2):**  Plants absorb this gas from the air through small pores on their leaves called stomata.
*   **Water (H2O):** Plants take up water from the soil through their roots.
*   **Sunlight:** The energy source for the entire process.

*   Photosynthesis takes place inside organelles called **chloroplasts** within plant cells.
*   Chloroplasts contain **chlorophyll**, a green pigment that absorbs sunlight. Chlorophyll is the key to capturing the light energy.
