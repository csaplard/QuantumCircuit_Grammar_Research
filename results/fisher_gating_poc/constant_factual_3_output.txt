A combustion engine, also known as an internal combustion engine, converts chemical energy (from fuel) into mechanical energy (to move a vehicle). Here's a breakdown of how it works, step by step, typically in a four-stroke engine (the most common type):

**1. Intake Stroke:**

*   **Piston Movement:** The piston moves *down* inside the cylinder.
*   **Valve Action:** The intake valve opens.
*   **What Happens:** A vacuum is created in the cylinder as the piston descends. This vacuum sucks in a mixture of air and fuel (gasoline or diesel) into the cylinder.  This is where the engine 'breathes in'.

**2. Compression Stroke:**

*   **Piston Movement:** The piston moves *up* inside the cylinder.
*   **Valve Action:** Both the intake and exhaust valves are closed.
*   **What Happens:** The piston compresses the air-fuel mixture, reducing its volume and increasing its pressure and temperature.  This compression is crucial for efficient combustion.

**3. Combustion (Power) Stroke:**

*   **Piston Movement:** The piston is forced *down* by the high-pressure air-fuel mixture.
