**1. Types of Vaccines:**

*   **Live-attenuated vaccines:** These use a weakened (attenuated) form of the living virus or bacteria. The weakened pathogen can still replicate, but it can't cause serious illness. (Examples: MMR, chickenpox, rotavirus)
*   **Inactivated vaccines:** These use a killed version of the pathogen. Because the pathogen is dead, it can't cause illness. (Examples: Polio (IPV), Hepatitis A, Flu shots)
