TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) and UDP (User Datagram Protocol) are both transport layer protocols used for sending data over a network, but they differ significantly in their characteristics and suitability for different applications. Here's a breakdown of the key differences:

**1. Connection-Oriented vs. Connectionless:**

* **TCP:**  TCP is **connection-oriented**. Before data transfer begins, a connection (a handshake) must be established between the sender and receiver. This ensures a reliable and ordered flow of data.  Think of it like making a phone call – you establish a connection before speaking.
* **UDP:** UDP is **connectionless**. It doesn't establish a connection beforehand. Data is simply sent without any prior setup.  It's like sending a postcard – you just drop it in the mail without confirming the recipient is ready.
