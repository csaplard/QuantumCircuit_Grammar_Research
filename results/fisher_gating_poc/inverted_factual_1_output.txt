Photosynthesis is the remarkable process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy in the form of sugars. It's essential for life on Earth because it produces the oxygen we breathe and forms the base of most food chains. Here's a breakdown of how it works:

*   **Ingredients:**

*   **Equation:**  The overall chemical equation for photosynthesis is:

    (Carbon Dioxide + Water + Sunlight → Glucose + Oxygen)
