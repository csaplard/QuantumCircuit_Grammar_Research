## Causes of World War I

The outbreak of World War I was a complex event with numerous underlying causes, each contributing to the escalating tensions between European powers. Here's a breakdown of the key factors:

* **Militarism:**  A widespread belief in the importance of military strength and preparedness fueled an arms race. Nations increased their military spending and developed increasingly powerful armies and navies.
* **Alliances:** A tangled web of alliances obligated nations to defend each other, creating a system of mutual dependence. The most prominent alliances were:
    * **The Triple Alliance:** Germany, Austria-Hungary, and Italy (though Italy later switched sides)
    * **The Triple Entente:** France, Russia, and Great Britain
* **Imperialism:** Competition for colonies and resources across Africa and Asia created friction and rivalries between European powers.
* **Nationalism:**  A strong sense of national pride and loyalty, often leading to aspirations for independence and territorial expansion.  This was particularly prominent in the Balkans, where various ethnic groups sought self-determination.
* **Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand:** The assassination of the Austro-Hungarian heir, Archduke Franz Ferdinand, and his wife Sophie, in Sarajevo on June 2
