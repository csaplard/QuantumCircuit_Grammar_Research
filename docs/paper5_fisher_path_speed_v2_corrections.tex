\subsection*{3. \S2.1 / \S2.2 --- sentence for $d=K(K{-}1)$}

\begin{verbatim}
Here d = K(K-1) counts the total number of free coordinates in the row-stochastic
model: each of the K rows lies on a (K-1)-simplex, and the Fisher-Rao metric is
taken with respect to the product of these K simplices (block-diagonal Fisher
information).
\end{verbatim}

\subsection*{4. \S3.3 --- after Table 1 (numeric support for $K{=}5,9$)}

\begin{verbatim}
For K=5 (26 exponential-fit readouts) the topology-level medians of A*tau are
54.13 (Snake, n=7), 49.00 (Block, n=13), and 56.79 (Bulk, n=6), with
CV = std/median within each topology of 0.22, 0.16, and 0.11 respectively.
For K=9 (25 exponential-fit readouts) the corresponding medians are 75.31,
73.50, and 74.30 (n=7,13,5) with CV 0.16, 0.17, and 0.07.
Thus the "topology independence within fixed K" statement is not special to
K=7; it is reproducible across alphabet sizes, with Snake rows typically
slightly noisier than Bulk in this CV sense.
\end{verbatim}

\subsection*{5. \S3.5 LLM --- replace ``10 non-pathological''}

\begin{verbatim}
The same analysis applied to LLM entropy time series (Qwen2.5-Coder:7B, K=7,
twelve experimental conditions) yields noisier profiles: the power-law tail
model is preferred by AIC in all twelve conditions, consistent with multi-scale
temporal structure documented in the Curvature Research Note.
We therefore do not interpret the exponential sub-parameters (tau, A, hence
A*tau) as primary estimands for LLMs; the reported median A*tau ~ 91.5
(and p = 0.11 vs. 65.97) is exploratory only and is not used in the main
Sycamore scaling analysis.
\end{verbatim}
